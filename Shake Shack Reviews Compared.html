How this was measured, and what it leaves out
Source
The public Google Maps review pages for both locations, sorted by newest and read in a browser on 9 November 2026 (London time). All-time star counts and topic counts are Google’s own.
Last 60 days
Every review newer than Google’s “2 months ago” label. Argyll St’s pages were also read back nine months; Leicester Sq receives so many reviews that 60 days was the comparable reach.
Dates
Google shows relative dates (“a month ago”), so monthly and weekly groupings are approximate.
Staff-name reviews
Reviews whose text includes a team member’s first name, found as names that recur across many reviews, nearly all of them 5★. Names are not shown here.
Themes
Keyword lists for praise and criticism of each theme, applied to review text (translated reviews in Google’s English translation). A guide to emphasis, not an exact count.
1–2★ issues
Each critical review with text was read and coded by hand; one review can count under several issues.
Shown-rating outlook
Assumes Google’s figure is the plain average of all reviews (it matches both locations today) and that the recent rating and pace hold.
Left out
Star-only reviews count in ratings but not in themes. A few very long reviews could only be read in part. Google marks its reviews as not verified. Other review sites were not included.
Updating the page
Every number and chart is drawn from the JSON data block near the end of this file. Replace the values there and the whole page, text included, follows.
